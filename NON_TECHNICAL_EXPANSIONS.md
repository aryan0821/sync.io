# 🌟 Expansions for Non-Technical Users

## Current State: Developer-Focused
The bot currently targets developers with GitHub, Linear, and code search. Here are strategic expansions to appeal to **non-coding users** (product managers, designers, marketers, sales, support, executives).

---

## 🎯 Top Priority Expansions (High Impact, Low Complexity)

### 1. **Calendar & Meeting Management** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why**: Everyone has meetings. This is universal.

**Features**:
- "What meetings do I have today?"
- "Schedule a meeting with [person] next Tuesday at 2pm"
- "What's my availability this week?"
- "Find time for a 30-minute meeting with the design team"
- "What meetings are coming up this afternoon?"
- "Cancel my 3pm meeting"

**Integrations**:
- Google Calendar
- Outlook/Exchange
- Calendly (for scheduling links)

**Value Proposition**:
- Zero context switching from Slack
- Natural language scheduling
- Team availability at a glance

**Example Use Cases**:
- Product Manager: "Schedule a sync with engineering about the Q4 roadmap"
- Sales: "When can I meet with [client] next week?"
- Executive: "What's my calendar look like today?"

---
